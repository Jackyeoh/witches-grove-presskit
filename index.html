About the game游戏介绍遊戲介紹

Choose a champion from a lineup of oddballs, and every run rolls them a fresh set of abilities. Send them into the ring with up to seven rivals. From round two, the lowest finishers are out. Outlast them all and win the Champion's Wish.

Nettle's market restocks every round. Teach a technique that rewires an ability, stack Training Tickets, pick up charms that come with a catch, and fill your belt with storms, frost, honey and exploding mushrooms.

Every run earns Petals and affinity with your fighter. Raise affinity to unlock new techniques, a second technique slot and five story chapters that end in your fighter's own wish.

Short Rumpus: six fighters and five rounds, a quick run. Grand Rumpus: eight fighters, seven rounds and Grove Games in the middle.

How AI was used关于 AI 的使用關於 AI 的使用

Witches' Grove is made by one person, and I want to be upfront about how it was built.

I used AI tools heavily during development. AI helped make the pixel-art sprites and effects (drawn as code) and the music, and it helped write much of the game's code.

AI made it possible for a solo developer to build something this size. It didn't decide what the game should be. Every fighter, wish, item and joke went through my hands. I played it over and over, threw out what didn't feel right, and sent things back again and again until they had the warmth and personality I wanted. The design, the world, the characters' stories and every final call are mine.

Nothing in the game is generated live while you play. Everything you see and hear was made and checked before release.

I hope you can feel the care that went into the Grove. Thank you for giving it a chance.

《满月大乱斗》由一个人制作完成，我想坦诚地说明它是怎么做出来的。

开发过程中我大量使用了 AI 工具。AI 帮忙制作了像素风角色与特效（以代码绘制）和音乐，也帮忙编写了游戏的大部分代码。

AI 让一名独立开发者能够做出这样规模的游戏，但它并没有决定这款游戏应该是什么样子。每一位斗士、每一个愿望、每一件道具和每一个笑话都经过我亲手打磨。我一遍又一遍地试玩，删掉感觉不对的部分，反复修改，直到它们拥有我想要的温度和个性。游戏的设计、世界观、角色故事以及每一个最终决定，都出自我手。

游戏中没有任何内容是在游玩时实时生成的。你看到和听到的一切，都在发售前制作并检查完毕。

希望你能感受到我倾注在林苑里的心意。谢谢你愿意给它一次机会。
